Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NPS Corpus Calculator ("this site," "we," "our") handles information when you use it. This site is an independent educational tool created by Jalaj Jain and is not affiliated with the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) or any government body.

1. Calculator inputs

All figures you enter into the calculator (age, contribution amount, expected returns, etc.) are processed entirely within your own browser. This data is not sent to, stored on, or seen by us, our servers, or any third party. Closing or refreshing the page clears it completely.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall visitor traffic to this site — such as how many people visit, which pages are viewed, and general device/location information (like city or country, not exact location). Google Analytics may set cookies on your browser to enable this.

This is aggregate, anonymized usage data. It does not include anything you type into the calculator itself. You can learn more about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Advertising

This site may in the future display advertisements served through Google AdSense or similar ad networks. These networks may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can review and adjust your ad personalization settings at Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies

Cookies used on this site are limited to those set by Google Analytics and (if enabled) Google AdSense, as described above. We do not set any first-party tracking cookies of our own.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties beyond what is described above for analytics and advertising purposes.

6. Children's privacy

This site is intended for general audiences researching retirement planning and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site evolves. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, you can reach out via the channels linked from the GS For Youth YouTube channel.
